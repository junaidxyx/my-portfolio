The Best Movies According to Reunes (2026 Edition)

My Top 3 Movies (2026)

Odyssey

This is easily one of my favorite movies of 2026. It's directed by Chris Nolan, has amazing scenes and is really entertaining to watch.

Obsession

One of the best horror films I've seen this year. Really unique and eerily creepy. Keeps you captivated throughout the entire movie. Jumpscares are good as well.

Resident Evil

Another really good horror film, but this one has a comedy touch to it (you'll know this if you've played RE). Great storytelling, but the ending kinda pissed me off.
